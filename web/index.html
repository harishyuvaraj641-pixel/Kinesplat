Under the hood

      
How kinesplat works

      
    
        
  1. 01
    Frames
    Photos or a video are resized, ordered and lightly prepared for reconstruction.
    2. 
        
  2. 02
    Cameras
    Poses come from bundled calibration, COLMAP when available, or kinesplat's built-in SfM with global rotation averaging.
    3. 
        
  3. 03
    Depth
    Depth Anything V2 Small — a pretrained ONNX model — predicts dense depth for every frame.
    4. 
        
  4. 04
    Splats
    Depth pixels become 3D Gaussians, optimised with a differentiable rasteriser on your GPU (or CPU) and exported as a standard 32-byte .splat.
    5. 
      

      
No cloud, nothing uploaded. The viewer is a custom WebGL2 instanced splat renderer with depth sorting.
